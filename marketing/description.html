In Little Vac, guide a small vacuum robot through 24 jobs and 50 areas in a workshop, an arcade, a greenhouse, and a rooftop after a party.

  • Upgrade your vacuum with a wider sweep, bigger bag, quicker wheels, and stronger suction. Buy upgrades between jobs. Later dust and stuck scraps need more suction.
  • Open hallways by cleaning each area to 100%. Later jobs span up to four areas. Walk back and forth; cleaned floors and your bag stay intact.
  • Find 24 optional treasures and unlock five robot colors. Finish the campaign to unlock Endless Shift: generated rooms with repeatable seeds.

Controls: Left-click to start, then move your mouse without holding, or use WASD / arrow keys. On touchscreens, tap Start and drag. Vacuuming is automatic. Follow the green arrow to empty your bag at the dock. Each area has one dock.

No lives or time limits; medals are optional. Coins save after the whole job. Quitting, restarting, reloading, or closing the page loses unfinished cleaning and coins. Progress saves in this browser. Pause and quit to upgrade early.
