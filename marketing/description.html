Clean rooms. Upgrade your vacuum.

Guide a little vacuum robot through 24 cleaning jobs and 50 areas. Pick up sawdust in a workshop, clear confetti from an arcade, tackle fallen leaves in a greenhouse, and clean up after a rooftop party.

Finish jobs to earn coins for a wider sweep, a bigger bag, quicker wheels, and stronger suction. There are no lives or time limits. Time medals are optional.

  • Connected rooms: later jobs have two, three, or four areas. Clean an area to 100% to unlock its hallway. The camera follows as you walk through, and you can go back whenever you need to.
  • A tougher clean: later dust and stuck scraps take more suction. Upgrades help you clean them faster.
  • Things to find: look for 24 optional treasures and unlock five robot colors.
  • Endless Shift: finish all 24 jobs to unlock generated rooms. Keep your upgrades and reuse a seed to play the same layout again.

How to play

  1. Start and steer. Left-click the room, then move your mouse without holding a button. Or press WASD / arrow keys. On touchscreens, tap Start, then drag the pad or room.
  2. Clean and empty. Vacuuming is automatic. Bag filling up? Follow the green arrow to the dock and park beside it. Each area has one dock; your bag comes with you through hallways.
  3. Finish the whole job. Clean every area to 100% to save your coins and unlock the next job. Buy upgrades between jobs. Yellow rings and pointers help you find the last pieces.

Press Esc or Pause for controls, settings, and job details. Opening menus keeps the job paused. To buy upgrades before finishing, use Pause → Quit & upgrade; this discards that job’s unfinished cleaning and coins.

Saving

Completed jobs, saved coins, upgrades, medals, treasures, and settings save in this browser. An unfinished job does not survive reloading or closing the page. Coins save only after every area in the job is clean, including coins from dirt already emptied at a dock. Saves do not transfer between browsers or hosting addresses. If saving is unavailable, the game shows a warning.
