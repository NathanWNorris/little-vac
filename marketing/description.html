A little mess. A lovely clean.

Sweep Shift is a cozy cleaning game about a tiny vacuum robot. Sweep through 24 jobs with 50 distinct areas, watch the floor reappear, and turn each finished clean into coins for a better robot. Finish the campaign to unlock Endless Shift.

Play in your browser. No lives or failed rooms. Take your time, or chase an optional time medal.

Every room is a fresh start

  • Clean 50 areas in 24 jobs across a workshop, an after-hours arcade, a greenhouse, and a rooftop party.
  • Follow the mess: scoop up crumbs, pull in fluttering confetti, clear dusty patches, and loosen stubborn scraps. Work around furniture and fans as new locations open.
  • Bigger jobs as you progress: later rooms have two, three, then four connected areas. Follow the amber arrow through each newly opened doorway. Denser dust and stuck leaves put your suction upgrades to work.
  • Build your favorite robot: buy five levels each of wider sweep, bigger bag, quicker wheels, and stronger suction. Choose any upgrade order.
  • Find little keepsakes: collect 24 optional treasures and unlock five robot colors.
  • Keep cleaning after the ending: Endless Shift creates fresh rooms from repeatable seeds, with all your upgrades intact.

How to play

  1. Start at the dock. Left-click inside the room, or press WASD / an arrow key to begin. Then move your mouse to steer without holding a button, or use WASD / arrow keys. On a touchscreen, drag the pad or room to steer. Vacuuming happens automatically.
  2. Dock to empty. Each area has one green collection station. Park beside it. A full bag stops vacuuming, but you can still move and collect keepsakes.
  3. Finish to earn and upgrade. Reach 100% in each area. In bigger jobs, follow the arrow through the NEXT AREA door and enter the next area from the opposite doorway without stopping. Finish every area to save your coins and unlock the next room. Visit Upgrades to see what you can afford.

Golden rings help you find the last bits of mess. Press Escape to pause. Sound, volume, and reduced motion are in Settings.

Pick up where you left off

Use Rooms to browse locations and replay your favorites. Opening menus pauses your current room; Resume room brings you straight back. Upgrades open after finishing a room, or after choosing Pause → Quit room & upgrade and confirming. Quitting loses only that room’s unfinished cleaning and coins.

Finished rooms, coins, upgrades, medals, and treasures save in this browser. Reloading, closing the game, or discarding a room loses that unfinished clean. Saves do not transfer between browsers or hosting addresses. If saving is blocked, the game warns you and remains playable for that session.
