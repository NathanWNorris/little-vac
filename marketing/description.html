A little mess. A lovely clean.

Guide a tiny vacuum robot through colorful rooms, gather the chaos, and watch a lovely clean floor emerge underneath. Empty your bag, pocket the coins, and give your robot a little tune-up before its next shift.

No lives. No rush. Just one satisfying little transformation after another.

  • 24 rooms across a workshop, an after-hours arcade, a greenhouse, and a rooftop party.
  • Crumbs, fluttering confetti, dusty patches, and stubborn scraps, with fans and furniture to work around.
  • 20 upgrade ranks, five robot shells, and 24 optional keepsakes.
  • Optional time medals. Simply finishing a room opens the next one.
  • A complete campaign and ending, followed by Endless Shift with repeatable room seeds.

Move. Gather. Glow.

WASD / arrow keys or hold the mouse button to move. On a touchscreen, drag the pad or room to steer. Your robot vacuums automatically. Stay beside a collection station to empty your bag.

At 80% clean, golden rings help you spot the remaining mess. At 95%, a final sweep takes care of the last crumbs. Escape pauses. Sound, volume, and reduced motion are in Settings.

Your little corner, saved

Completed rooms, purchases, medals, and treasures save in this browser. Unfinished rooms restart when you return. Saves do not transfer between browsers or hosting addresses. If saving is blocked, a warning appears and the game remains playable for that session.

Made with care

A game by Nathan Norris, developed with AI assistance for code, design iteration, writing, and testing. Original artwork is drawn in Canvas and SVG; sound effects are synthesized with Web Audio. No external runtime libraries or asset downloads. Screenshots show actual gameplay.
